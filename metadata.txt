about=brmangue-qgis is a QGIS interface for the BR-MANGUE simulation model, built on the DisSModel ecosystem (LambdaGeo / UFMA). It submits jobs to a remote DisSModel Platform server, polls for completion, downloads the GeoTIFF result, and applies automatic band symbology (land use, soil, elevation) directly in the QGIS canvas. No local installation of dissmodel or brmangue-dissmodel is required — the science runs on the server.
